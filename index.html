Companion for GW2

Policy and disclaimer

Unofficial fan app

Companion for GW2 is an unofficial app made by a fan. It is not made, endorsed, sponsored or supported by ArenaNet, LLC or NCSOFT Corporation, and it is not affiliated with them in any way. It does not work with, or on behalf of, ArenaNet: it only reads the public Guild Wars 2 API that ArenaNet offers to everyone.

Trademarks and game content

©ArenaNet, LLC. All rights reserved. NCSOFT, ArenaNet, Guild Wars, Guild Wars 2, GW2 and all associated logos, designs and composite marks are trademarks or registered trademarks of NCSOFT Corporation. The game images and data shown in the app belong to ArenaNet and are used under ArenaNet’s content terms of use. All other trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Your API key

Your API key is stored only on this device, in the app’s private storage, and is left out of backups and device transfers. It is sent only to the official Guild Wars 2 API (api.guildwars2.com), to read the account information its permissions allow. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Remove the account in the app to delete the key, and revoke it at any time at account.arena.net.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. Your watchlists, goals, settings and pictures stay on this device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Services the app connects to

Only ArenaNet’s own services (the Guild Wars 2 API, and its image and map-tile servers) and the official Guild Wars 2 Wiki, for pictures and some game data. As with any website, these services see your device’s IP address when the app contacts them, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Internet, to reach the services above. Camera, only while you scan an API key’s QR code: the scan happens on the device and the image is not kept or sent anywhere. Notifications, exact alarms and full-screen alerts, for the reminders and event alarms you turn on. Starting at boot, to set your reminders again after a restart. Pictures you choose for an avatar or a character are copied into the app’s private storage, and a scene is saved to your gallery only when you export it.

Support purchase

The optional one-time support purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details: the app only learns whether the purchase is owned, to unlock the supporters’ themes.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy is published on the policy page, with its date. Questions can be raised as an issue on the policy’s GitHub page.
